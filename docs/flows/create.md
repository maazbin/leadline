# Create

Folder: `src/create/`. Page: `/create`. Lane: `next_action` with `lane` `create`.

## Done

A draft exists for a channel on the active campaign. With Full power on, the desk publishes a LinkedIn or X draft after the words are in the box. With Full power off, the person marks it published after they post it.

## Steps

1. Wait until a campaign is active. Content does not start the campaign.
2. `create_content` for one channel that has no live draft. Use only the campaign and the profile.
3. With Full power on, stop. The desk publishes that LinkedIn or X draft. Do not post a second copy.
4. With Full power off, stop. The person opens the composer, posts it, and marks it published.

## Must not

Add a tool that sets status to `published` without the browser click. Block the Reach lane on a missing post. Invent proof, prices, or customers.
